Common questions

Straight answers. Better decisions.

Where does Vidhi Transport operate?

Our published network includes 14 branch locations across Gujarat and Maharashtra, serving nearby industrial clusters subject to lane and vehicle availability.

Do you offer both Part Load and Full Truckload?

Yes. PTL suits smaller accepted consignments; FTL provides dedicated vehicle planning for larger or time-sensitive loads.

Can you move packaged chemical drums and IBCs?

Certain packaged industrial cargo may be accepted after review of material classification, packaging, documents, route and safety requirements.

How do I get a freight quotation?

Share collection and delivery locations, cargo description, packaging, weight, dimensions, handling needs and preferred date using our enquiry form.
