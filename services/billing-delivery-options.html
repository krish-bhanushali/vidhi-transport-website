Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

What does DACC mean?

DACC means Delivery Against Consignee Copy, subject to the booking terms agreed with the operations team.

What information is required for a quote?

Pickup and destination addresses, precise cargo description, packaging, total weight, dimensions, preferred date and applicable documentation.

Is this service available in every city?

Coverage and capacity depend on the precise origin, destination, cargo, timing and operating conditions. Ask the relevant branch to confirm.
