Work that matters

The shipment moves because people notice the details.

Transport depends on teams who can understand a requirement, coordinate across locations, keep records clear and respond calmly when conditions change. Vidhi Transport welcomes profiles from people who value accountability and customer service.

We consider experience from logistics as well as transferable skills from industrial operations, fleet support, customer coordination, sales, accounts and administration. What we value

Good logistics habits travel well.

01

Ownership

Take responsibility for the information, follow-up and commitments connected to your work.

02

Respect

Communicate professionally with customers, drivers, vendors, colleagues and branch teams.

03

Accuracy

Treat shipment details, contact information and operating records with care.

04

Adaptability

Think clearly when routes, timing, capacity or customer requirements change.

Useful details

Career application questions.

Please rely only on contact details published on the Vidhi Transport website when discussing an application.

Are there current vacancies at Vidhi Transport?

This page invites expressions of interest and does not represent a specific vacancy unless Vidhi Transport confirms one directly. Suitable profiles may be reviewed when a relevant requirement arises.

How should I apply?

Email your current CV to customercare@vidhitransport.com with the role area and preferred work location in the subject line. Include your phone number, relevant experience and availability.

Which locations can I mention in my application?

You may mention a preferred location in Gujarat or Maharashtra. Opportunities depend on confirmed staffing requirements at the head office, branches and operating locations.

Does Vidhi Transport charge candidates a recruitment fee?

Candidates should verify every recruitment conversation through Vidhi Transport's published customer-care number or email before sharing documents. No fee commitment is stated on this page.
