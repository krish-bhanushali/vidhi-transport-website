Choose the right movement

Nine services. One coordinated network.

The right freight option depends on shipment volume, route, cargo type, handling requirements and dispatch schedule. Our operations team helps match those details to a practical movement plan.

01 · Dedicated capacity

Full Truckload Transport

A dedicated vehicle for high-volume, scheduled or time-sensitive industrial consignments requiring direct movement planning.

  • Dedicated vehicle for one consignment
  • Suitable for plant and warehouse dispatch
  • Planned point-to-point coordination
View FTL details
02 · Flexible consolidation

Part Load Transport

Consolidated road freight for smaller and recurring industrial consignments that do not require an entire vehicle.

  • Cost-conscious shared vehicle capacity
  • Useful for regular smaller dispatches
  • Lane-based consolidation planning
View PTL details
03 · Return movement

Reverse Logistics

Coordinated return movement for rejected goods, reusable packaging and industrial materials, subject to cargo and route requirements.

  • Rejected and return-goods coordination
  • Reusable packaging return support
  • Route-appropriate movement planning
Explore Reverse Logistics
04 · Industrial cargo focus

Chemical & Industrial Freight

Road freight coordination for packaged chemical drums, carboys, IBCs, industrial inputs, packaging, machinery and finished goods.

  • Packaged drums, carboys and IBCs
  • Industrial inputs and finished goods
  • Documentation-led cargo coordination
Explore Industrial Freight
05 · End-to-end coordination

First-Mile & Last-Mile Support

Pickup and delivery coordination between factories, warehouses, branches and final industrial destinations where operationally available.

  • Factory and warehouse pickup support
  • Branch and network handoff coordination
  • Destination delivery where available
Explore First & Last Mile
06 · Milestone-led coordination

Project Cargo Transport

Planned movement of industrial machinery, equipment and multi-stage project consignments with route, lifting, site-access and schedule coordination.

  • Item-wise cargo and movement planning
  • Site, vehicle and lifting coordination
  • Phased dispatch and milestone support
Explore Project Cargo
07 · Specialised movement planning

ODC & Construction Cargo

Movement planning for over-dimensional equipment, construction machinery, fabricated structures and project-related industrial cargo.

  • Route and access assessment
  • Vehicle and equipment planning
  • Permit coordination where required
Explore ODC Transport
08 · Port-linked movement

Container Transport to Ports

Container movement coordination between industrial locations and JNPT (Nhava Sheva), Hazira and Mundra, subject to terminal conditions and vehicle availability.

  • Factory-to-port and port-to-factory movement
  • Loaded or empty container coordination
  • Schedule and document coordination
Explore Container Transport
09 · Storage and onward dispatch

Warehousing & Distribution

Practical warehousing, consolidation, inventory staging and onward distribution for accepted packaged industrial goods.

  • Inward receipt and agreed storage support
  • Consolidation and dispatch staging
  • FTL and PTL distribution coordination
Explore Warehousing

Flexible commercial support

Billing and delivery options built around your process.

Choose an agreed freight-payment method and delivery arrangement to match your purchase, dispatch and consignee process.

01Freight payment & billing

  • Freight Paid
  • Freight To Pay
  • Account / Contract Billing

02Delivery arrangements

  • Door to Door
  • Godown to Door
  • Godown to Godown

03Controlled delivery options

  • Delivery Against Consignee Copy (DACC)
  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • Agreed combinations

Options can be combined, for example To Pay with Door-to-Door delivery or Account Billing with DACC, subject to customer agreement, cargo, route, documentation and operational confirmation.
