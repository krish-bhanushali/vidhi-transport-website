Trust and responsibility

Three credentials presented with the right context.

Vidhi Transport highlights its Go Green initiative, IBA approval and ISO certification. Request the current supporting documents when completing procurement or vendor checks.

  • Review the current document and validity period.
  • Match the legal entity name before relying on a credential.
  • Publish the exact ISO standard only after checking the certificate.
