Useful details

A quote is only as precise as the brief.

  • Use exact PIN codes for both ends.
  • State actual gross weight and package dimensions.
  • Declare drums, carboys, regulated or hazardous material accurately.
  • Tell us whether cargo is stackable.
  • Add forklift, crane, dock and vehicle-access constraints.

Helpful answers

Contact and quotation questions

What details are required for a transport quote?

Share exact origin and destination, material, packaging, package count, total weight, dimensions, ready date, loading method, delivery terms and any special handling or documentation needs.

Can I request part load and full truckload options?

Yes. Choose ‘Please recommend’ when the shipment could suit either option. The team can assess the movement using the actual cargo and route details.

Does sending this enquiry confirm price or vehicle placement?

No. The enquiry gives Vidhi Transport the information needed to review the requirement. Price, service acceptance, vehicle placement and schedule require separate confirmation.

How quickly can I contact customer care?

Customer care is listed as available 8:00 AM–10:00 PM IST, daily. You can call +91 98253 21589 or email customercare@vidhitransport.com.
