Connect each end of the route

Pickup and delivery support around the main movement.

First-mile support coordinates an accepted consignment from the origin facility to the relevant branch, hub or onward movement. Last-mile support coordinates the final leg from the network to the agreed consignee location.

Availability depends on local coverage, vehicle access, cargo, timing and delivery conditions. Clear contact details and site instructions help prevent avoidable delays at either end.

What this service can include

  • Factory and warehouse pickup coordination
  • Branch, hub and godown handoff support
  • Industrial consignee delivery where available
  • Door-to-Door and Godown-to-Door planning
  • Contact and site-access instruction coordination
  • Connection with accepted FTL, PTL or return freight

Often suitable for

  • Factory pickups
  • Warehouse dispatches
  • Branch-connected freight
  • Industrial consignee delivery

A clear operating sequence

How we plan this movement.

The exact operating plan varies by cargo, route and availability, but every enquiry begins with verified shipment details.

  1. 01

    Check both locations

    Share complete addresses, contacts, timings and vehicle-access information.

  2. 02

    Connect the movement

    The team aligns pickup or delivery with the relevant FTL, PTL, branch or hub plan.

  3. 03

    Coordinate handover

    Collection and final delivery are managed against agreed site and consignee instructions.

Important: First-mile and last-mile service is subject to local coverage, cargo acceptance, vehicle access, time restrictions and operational availability.

Service questions

Frequently asked questions.

These answers explain the usual planning approach. Share the exact cargo and route for a shipment-specific response.

What is first-mile transport support?

First-mile support covers the accepted pickup leg from a factory, warehouse or shipper location into the planned logistics movement.

What is last-mile industrial delivery?

Last-mile support covers the final accepted delivery leg from a branch, hub or godown to the industrial consignee location.

Is Door-to-Door delivery available everywhere?

Availability varies by route, locality, cargo, vehicle access and service conditions. Share the full addresses so the team can check coverage.

Which location details help with planning?

Provide addresses, landmarks, contact people, operating hours, loading or unloading access and any appointment restrictions.
