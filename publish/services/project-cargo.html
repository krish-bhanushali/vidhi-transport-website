One movement plan, many dependencies

Project cargo succeeds when every handoff is planned before dispatch.

Industrial project cargo is more than booking a truck. A machinery movement can depend on equipment dimensions, lifting points, site-entry restrictions, vehicle selection, loading windows, unloading readiness and the order in which individual units must reach the destination.

Vidhi Transport reviews accepted project requirements as a coordinated operating scope. The team aligns the route, vehicle or trailer, responsible contacts and movement milestones for Gujarat–Maharashtra industrial corridors. When a unit is over-dimensional, its ODC feasibility and statutory requirements are reviewed separately within the wider project plan.

Every quotation remains specific to the actual cargo. Route surveys, permits, escorts, cranes, rigging, labour, transit commitments and site work are included only when expressly confirmed in the agreed scope.

What this service can include

  • Cargo list, verified dimensions, weights and support points
  • Origin, destination, route and site-access review
  • Vehicle, trailer and load-securing requirement planning
  • Lifting, crane, rigging and labour responsibility alignment
  • Phased dispatch, delivery sequence and milestone coordination
  • Project contacts, reporting points and exception communication

A milestone-led operating sequence

How a project movement is prepared.

The sequence is tailored to the cargo and site, but the movement should not begin until the operating inputs and responsibilities are clear.

  1. 01

    Define the scope

    Confirm every cargo unit, dimension, weight, sequence, site, target date and responsible project contact.

  2. 02

    Engineer the movement

    Review route and access feasibility, vehicles, load support, lifting interfaces and requirements for any ODC unit.

  3. 03

    Execute milestones

    Coordinate accepted dispatch stages, operating contacts, progress updates and destination readiness against the agreed plan.

Important: Service is subject to verified technical details, route and site feasibility, statutory requirements, equipment availability and a written, agreed operating scope.

Helpful answers

Project cargo questions

What is project cargo transport?

Project cargo transport is a planned movement for industrial machinery, equipment or multiple related consignments where vehicle selection, site readiness, lifting responsibilities, schedules and milestones must be coordinated as one operating scope.

What information is needed for a project cargo quotation?

Share the cargo list, dimensions and weight of every unit, drawings or photographs, lifting points, origin and destination access, loading and unloading arrangements, target dates and any phased-delivery sequence.

Can a project cargo movement include ODC items?

Yes, an accepted project movement can include over-dimensional cargo. ODC items require a separate review of exact dimensions, route feasibility, vehicle or trailer needs, permits, escorts and applicable operating requirements.

Does Vidhi Transport arrange cranes and lifting equipment?

Lifting, crane, rigging and labour responsibilities are agreed before booking. Availability and responsibility depend on the cargo, site, location and confirmed project scope; they should never be assumed from the transport quotation alone.
