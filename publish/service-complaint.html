Helpful answers

Service complaint questions

Which reference should I include in a transport complaint?

Include the LR number exactly as printed on the transport receipt. Also provide the origin, destination, issue type and an authorised contact who can answer follow-up questions.

What information helps investigate damage or shortage?

Describe what was received, when it was noticed, the package condition and the expected resolution. Keep the LR, invoice, delivery records and relevant photographs available if the team requests them through an authorised channel.

Does submitting the form guarantee a claim or resolution?

No. Submission registers the information for review. Liability, claim eligibility, required documents, timelines and resolution depend on the shipment records, applicable terms and investigation.

What should I do for an urgent delivery issue?

Submit complete details, then call customer care with the LR number. Do not submit passwords, banking credentials or sensitive originals through the public form.
