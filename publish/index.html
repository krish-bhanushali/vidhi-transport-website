Vidhi Transport Co.

Built around the industrial corridors we know.

For more than 25 years, Vidhi Transport Co. has supported industrial freight movement across Gujarat and Maharashtra through practical planning, direct coordination and dependable road logistics.

Our work begins with the shipment itself: where it must be collected, how it should be handled, which movement type fits, and who needs a clear update. From packaged chemical drums, carboys and IBCs to pharmaceutical cartons, industrial packaging and machinery, we plan movement around real dispatch requirements.

The network combines local branch contacts with full truckload, part load and specialised freight coordination. That corridor familiarity helps us discuss real operating constraints around industrial estates, plant schedules, delivery points, ports and warehouses.

How it works

From requirement to movement.

A short, accountable sequence: every enquiry begins with verified shipment details.

Start with step one
  1. 01

    Share the requirement

    Tell us the pickup point, destination, cargo type, packaging, approximate weight and desired dispatch date. Exact PIN codes and site constraints help the team answer usefully.

  2. 02

    Plan the movement

    The operating team checks the suitable service (PTL, FTL or specialised), vehicle suitability, route coverage, documentation and commercial terms before acceptance.

  3. 03

    Pickup and loading

    We lift immediately, or schedule the movement for the next available dispatch window, depending on your requirement and vehicle availability.

  4. 04

    Movement and communication

    Dispatch and branch contacts remain available throughout the movement, and the LR number can be checked against the live ERP record on our tracking page.

  5. 05

    Delivery and documentation

    Delivery follows the agreed arrangement: door, godown, DACC or COD where agreed. Shipment reports and MIS reports are available to help customers monitor freight activity.

Which regions does Vidhi Transport serve?

Vidhi Transport serves industrial corridors across Gujarat and Maharashtra, including Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Ankleshwar, Jhagadia, Saykha, Panoli, Dahej, Sachin, Kadodara, Vapi, Umergaon, Daman, Bhiwandi, Rabale, Navi Mumbai, Ambernath, JNPT and Mundra.

What is the difference between full truckload and part truckload transport?

Full truckload provides a dedicated vehicle for one consignment. Part truckload consolidates smaller consignments so a customer does not need to reserve the entire vehicle.

What types of industrial cargo does Vidhi Transport handle?

We coordinate movement for packaged chemical drums, carboys, IBCs, pharmaceutical cartons, industrial packaging, raw materials, finished goods and machinery, subject to cargo documentation and handling requirements.

Does Vidhi Transport provide shipment reports and MIS reports?

Yes. Shipment reporting and MIS reports are available to help customers monitor freight movement and operational activity.

Does Vidhi Transport provide reverse logistics for rejected goods?

Yes. We coordinate cost-conscious return movement for rejected goods and other reverse-logistics requirements, subject to the cargo and route.

Does Vidhi Transport support other transport booking and last-mile delivery?

Yes. Where operationally available, we can store finished goods and coordinate onward booking or last-mile delivery according to the destination and timeline.

How can I request a freight quotation?

Call Vidhi Transport, use the website enquiry form, or send the pickup location, destination, cargo details and preferred dispatch date through WhatsApp.

Where can I verify Vidhi Transport's credentials?

Visit our Credentials page for information about the Go Green initiative, Indian Banks' Association approval and ISO certification, or contact us for current supporting documents.
